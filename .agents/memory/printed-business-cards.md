---
name: Printed employee business cards
description: Employee business cards are physical print pieces, not interactive UI.
---

Employee business cards are printed, so phone numbers, email addresses, and website addresses must appear as static contact information rather than clickable controls. Avoid link anchors, `tel:`/`mailto:` behavior, hover states, and arrows that imply clicking.

**Why:** The user clarified that these designs are printed business cards and do not need clickable behavior.

**How to apply:** When designing or updating employee business cards, preserve the readable contact details but remove interaction semantics and click affordances.
